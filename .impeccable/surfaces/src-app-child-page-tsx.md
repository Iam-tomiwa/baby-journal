# Journal surfaces

Scope: the shelf (`/`), a child's journal (`/[child]`), and the parents' desk (`/parents`).
Mode: Experience for the shelf and journal; Operate for the parents' desk, which inherits the same world at lower volume.

Audience and job: family and friends read one child's journal from a shared link; parents add a memory in under a minute, mostly on a phone.
Content: photos, videos, milestone firsts, written notes, a mixtape of YouTube or Spotify links.
Constraints: public reading, passcode writing; several children; smooth motion on phone, tablet and desktop; reduced motion honoured.
Memorable moment: the browser window is the closed book, and scrolling swings the cover open.
Unresolved: editing an existing entry; choosing the cover photo by hand.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The window is a cloth-bound baby journal. You open its cover and read sheets of coloured sugar paper, one per age, with prints taped down by hand. It refuses the equal-card photo grid and the cream-paper memory app.

OWN-WORLD: Bookcloth covers in tomato, cobalt, marigold, leaf or plum with a stamped chunky title and a round die-cut photo window. Inside, full-bleed sugar-paper sheets in sky, butter, rose, mint and lilac, cut apart with pinking-shear edges. White-bordered prints under washi tape, die-cut stickers with a white edge, rounded date cards, lined note slips, fabric index tabs, a navy album page of cassettes. Caprasimo for display, Gochi Hand for anything a parent wrote, Schibsted Grotesk for controls. Ink is navy, never grey.

STORY: A visitor sees whose book this is, opens it, and reads a childhood in order of age. A parent sees the entry they are making exactly as it will sit on the page before they stick it in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The whole viewport is the cover in the child's colour: stitched spine down the left edge, photo window upper centre, the child's name stamped very large beneath it, birth date in hand below. One action, "Open the journal", sits under the name; quiet links to the shelf and the parents' desk sit in the top corners.

FORM: A physical baby journal, as pinned by the owner. seed: none (pinned) "It's meant to simulate a physical baby journal"; "a joyful, animated page where the baby's memory show"; reading model confirmed as "Cover, then scrolling chapters".

Signature interaction: scroll (or the button) swings the full-window cover open on its spine to reveal the nameplate page. Motion grammar: things are placed, not faded. Prints settle onto the sheet with a small turn, tape presses down after them, stickers are slapped on; opening a print lifts that same print off the page into the reader.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
